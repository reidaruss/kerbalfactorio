## 1. Mission
Procedurally generate believable, deterministic-from-seed planets and moons: terrain, biomes, resource deposits, and points of interest — streamable as LOD chunks, deformable where needed, and cheap to persist (seed + diffs).

## 2. Scope & owned subsystems
- **Cubed-sphere quadtree terrain** (KSP PQS-style) — six quadtree-subdivided cube faces on a sphere; noise-driven heightfield; streaming LOD.
- **Biomes & surface materials** — climate/latitude/altitude driven; feeds rendering materials + gameplay science.
- **Resource deposits** — seeded node placement; per-body resource identity (this moon = X-rich) to drive logistics/exploration.
- **Voxel/SDF deformation patches** — overlay only where players dig; keep heightfield for the untouched bulk.
- **POI / structure placement** — ruins, anomalies, loot sites (content authored by [gameplay](gameplay.md); placement here).
- **Body parameters** — radius, gravity (μ), atmosphere profile, rotation — shared with physics/rendering.
- **Non-goals:** rendering the terrain (→ rendering consumes our data); what loot/quests POIs contain (→ gameplay).

## 4. Architecture & approach
- **Terrain:** noise stack (continents → mountains → detail) evaluated per cubed-sphere quad at the LOD needed by camera distance; quads stream in/out. Everything derives from `(bodySeed, faceId, quadPath)` → fully regenerable, so the natural world costs ~zero to save.
- **Deformation:** when a player digs, instantiate a voxel/SDF patch seeded from the heightfield at that location; store the *delta* only. Rendering & physics query "is there a patch here?" before falling back to heightfield. (v1 may stub this; keep the seam in the API.)
- **Deposits/POIs:** a deterministic placement pass over each body (Poisson-disk / hashed-grid by seed) yields node positions + types; gameplay binds content. Depletion/looted flags are diffs.
- **Floating-origin aware:** all queries accept/return data in core-engine's coordinate/frame terms; chunk origins are 64-bit, local meshes are near-origin.

## 7. Open questions & risks
- **R1:** Heightfield↔voxel-patch seam (rendering + collision continuity at dig boundaries). **API seam now built** (Spike 1 §4.4: `bFromVoxelPatch` in every result struct, `ApplyPatchOverride` no-op stub — callers branch on it today); implementation deferred to Phase 4 (D-005). *(Spike 1 WR4)*
- **R2:** Noise cost vs streaming budget at low LOD over large bodies. **Mitigated by design:** LOD octave-gating (Spike 1 §2) + per-tick generation budget (§3.2); validate gate WV8. Still open until measured in-engine.
- **R3 (Admin Q4):** keep voxel door open without paying for it in v1. **Settled** by R1 seam above.
- **R4 (new, Spike 1 WR3 → Admin):** numeric body constants (radius/μ/SOI/spin, Spike 1 §5) are **shared** with core-engine's `FReferenceFrame` but core-engine pinned only the *shape*, not values. World-gen proposed Forge (planet) / Cinder (moon) numbers; **Admin to confirm single source of truth** so the two domains don't diverge. No contract conflict — value agreement only.
- **R5 (new, Spike 1 WR5 → physics):** physics collision against heightfield via **analytic queries** (built) vs **baked per-quad collision meshes** — negotiate with physics. Contract supports both; collision-mesh emission can be added to `FTerrainChunk` later without breaking callers.
- **R7 (new, 2026-07-26, the FIFTH instance of the surface class — Admin asked for a position):** the ONE oracle answers with **two SHAPES**. `solidCell` is a binary 1 m occupancy grid (a cell is solid when its CENTRE is at or below the designed surface); `surfaceHeight` is a smooth continuous field. A binary lattice can only approximate a smooth surface to within half a cell diagonal, so the shell stands up to **0.87 m proud** of the walkable ground (measured: air 0.15 m above the surface reads solid on 60.6% of walking ticks — ARCHITECTURE 15.2 item 93). **Position: neither offered option is right.** Changing the quantisation in `surface_field.h` CANNOT succeed, because the mismatch is not a bad threshold, it is inherent to the representation: any rule leaves a boundary layer up to one cell thick that one shape claims and the other does not, so moving the threshold moves WHERE the disagreement is and not WHETHER it exists (and it would re-baseline every voxel pin for nothing). Per-consumer reconciliation is what produced five surfaces: `VoxelCollision.solidForWalker` is correct for the walker and is the SIXTH definition unless it is promoted. **The third option: name both shapes in the oracle and make the choice a parameter rather than a rediscovery.** `surface_field.h` should publish `solidCell` (the VOXEL shell — what the mesher draws and what a dig carves; you must draw whole cells) alongside a `solidBelowSurface` (the shell INTERSECTED with the smooth surface — what a body occupies), with a header note stating the bound explicitly, and a ctest that ASSERTS it: for a sample of directions the two answers may differ by at most half a cell diagonal. A property test on the SIZE of a known disagreement is what turns "silently 0.87 m" into "loudly, at most 0.87 m". **WG-22 hit the same class from the other side:** `levelArea` decides per cell CENTRE while `derivedRaisingAt`/`derivedLoweringAt` probe per COLUMN in 1 m steps, so a rim column is legitimately half cut and reports its original height (15.2 item 99). Same root, opposite sign. Not changed this pass (Admin did not ask for a core change); proposed for the next.
- **R6 (new, Spike 1 WR1):** cube-face-corner seam determinism under tangent-warp float error — mitigated by canonical-edge ownership (lower faceId owns a shared edge so both faces read identical doubles); validate WV2.
